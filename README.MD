# J. Hynes & R. Zuk
# Entropy, 2026
# mixed-media, 12” x 24” x 8”

### purpose: an interactive autodestructive installation that invites the community to reflect on the degree of randomness surrounding us.

### how to: (i) write whatever your heart desires and place it into the woven sculpture, (ii) on the night of HeARTburn, press the buttons to activate and influence Entropy’s disorder.

### expected outcome: the heart sculpture and its contents will be burned at a private ceremony.
